Chapter 1

The Secret Admirer

Our story begins in 2020, in Jude’s final year of college at PUP Manila. Nica walked into his section on a mission, campaigning for president of the PUP ECE student organization. The moment she opened her mouth to speak, Jude was captivated — there was a fire in the way she carried herself that he couldn’t look away from. Even before he realized he was falling for her, a part of his heart already belonged to her.

A year later, in 2021, life gave him a second chance. Nica, now in her own final year, posted a page where anyone could leave her an anonymous message. Jude had been quietly admiring her from afar, so he took his shot — writing her a message full of Pokémon references, with a quiet confession tucked inside: he had a crush on her. It was his way of testing the waters, even from all the way in Ireland, to see if he stood a chance of winning her heart.

Chapter 2

Finally Official

That message didn’t stay a secret for long. Nica’s org mate, Pol, knew who the mysterious “Pokémon guy” really was, and told her that this guy was her boyfriend standard. That was all it took to spark her curiosity, and she set out to find out who he was.

Soon after, Jude started posting Pokémon stories one after another, hoping to catch her attention like little pieces of clickbait. It worked — Nica replied to his story about a Pokémon mug, and they hit it off right away. By late October 2021, they were finally talking.

Their connection only grew from there. In the first week of April 2022, Jude flew home from Ireland to the Philippines to finally meet her, and on April 28, 2022, they made it official — two people choosing each other and saying yes to the adventure ahead.

Chapter 3

Love Across the Miles

Being official was one thing — but their next challenge was an ocean apart. From 2022 to 2023, Jude was working at the Analog Devices site in Limerick, Ireland, while Nica held down her own job at the Analog Devices site in Cavite, Philippines. For almost a year, they kept their love alive through late-night calls, countdowns, and the promise of a future together. Distance tried to come between them, but it never stood a chance.

Chapter 4

Together at Last — Ireland, 2023

In 2023, the long-distance chapter finally came to an end. Nica was transferred to the Analog Devices site in Limerick, and she crossed the world to be with Jude in Ireland at last. She brought Raffy and Maxine along with her, and not long after, they adopted Tom — completing their little home with three very spoiled fur babies who immediately took over the house, and their hearts.

Chapter 5

The Proposal — London

A year into life together in Ireland, love reached its next milestone. On their second anniversary, April 28, 2024, Jude whisked Nica away to the city of London — and amid the lights of the city, he got down on one knee. Through happy tears and a resounding “yes,” two hearts made it official: forever was finally on the horizon.

It was just the two of them in their hotel room that night. They had flown in from the Philippines after living out their dream trip to Japan, and London was only meant to be a stopover — their flight to Ireland wasn’t until the next day. No one could have guessed that a simple layover would become the place where forever began.

Chapter 6

Wanderlust Together

Now engaged, they set out to see the world together. Two hearts, one passport at a time — from the rolling green hills of Ireland to breathtaking European cities, and back to the beautiful islands of the Philippines, Jude and Nica turned exploring the world into one of their greatest love languages. Jude truly loves the hunt for cheap flights, finding a great deal is one of his simple joys on every trip they plan together, and through it all, Nica isn’t just his passenger princess, but his travel princess too.

Chapter 7

Building Our Future

Between adventures, they were also building a life to come home to. They dreamed it, they worked for it, and they made it happen — together. In March 2025, their dream car, a Tesla, pulled into the driveway in Limerick, the very same month Jude landed a new job at Cadence in Cork. By July 2025, they had bought their house in Limerick, turning it into a home truly their own, and that October, Jude proudly graduated with his Master’s degree.

The momentum carried into 2026. In January, they bought a family car in the Philippines, and by May, Nica had earned a well-deserved promotion at work. Then in July 2026, they finally got the keys to their condo unit in Mandaluyong — giving them two places to call home: one in the heart of Ireland, and one in the Philippines. Every milestone, every memory, built side by side.

And now, after years of growing, loving, and building a beautiful life together, they’re finally ready to make it official and legal. In January 2027, Jude and Nica will say “I do” and begin the most exciting chapter yet: building a family of their very own.
